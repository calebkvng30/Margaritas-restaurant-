The Room

Chandeliers over tufted leather. A grill out back.

Every table sits under low brass light — a chandelier at the front of house, a low copper-slat glow through the back dining room. Deep buttoned-leather booths run the length of the main wall, and the words painted above them say plainly what the kitchen's built on:

"Fine food, love and family."

The Cellar

A wine room, not a wine list.

Two full walls of racked reds behind glass, a lower tier of chilled whites and MCC, and a case history that's picked up two Best of Bloemfontein wine awards along the way. Ask your server to open the doors.
